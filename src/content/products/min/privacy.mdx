When you use Min, your bookmarks and settings stay in your browser. This policy
explains what Min processes, when it connects to outside services, and the
choices you have.

## Information Min handles

Min accesses your bookmarks so you can view, search, and manage them from your
new tab page. Your bookmarks and browsing data are not sent to a server
operated by Min.

Your settings, selected city, and cached weather are stored locally in Chrome.
Min only sends information outside your browser when you choose to use weather
or allow usage analytics, as described below.

## Why Min uses Chrome permissions

Min uses each permission only for the feature described below:

- **`bookmarks`**: Lets you show, search, add, edit, remove, and reorder Chrome
  bookmarks.
- **`favicon`**: Lets you recognize bookmarks by showing each site's icon.
- **`fontSettings`**: Lets you choose from fonts available in your browser.
- **`search`**: Lets you search with your selected search provider when your
  query is not a bookmark or web address.
- **`storage`**: Keeps your new tab preferences, selected city, and cached
  weather in Chrome.
- **`geolocation`**: Gets your coordinates only when you ask Min to show
  weather for your current location.

## Weather is optional

When you search for a city, Min sends the search text to Open-Meteo. When you
use your current location, Min sends your coordinates to BigDataCloud to find
your city and to Open-Meteo to retrieve the weather.

These services handle that information under their own privacy policies. Min
does not use it for any other purpose.

## Usage analytics is your choice

Min asks before collecting usage analytics. Nothing is sent to PostHog unless
you choose to allow it.

If you opt in, Min sends limited usage information to PostHog, including a
randomly generated identifier, the extension version, browser and operating
system information, language, screen size, network information such as your IP
address, and the names of features you use.

Min does not send your bookmarks, bookmark titles, URLs, search queries,
location, or weather data for analytics.

## Your privacy controls

You can turn usage analytics on or off at any time in **Min → Settings →
General → Privacy**. Turning it off stops future analytics collection.

To request deletion of previously collected analytics data, open the same
Privacy screen, copy your Analytics ID, and email it to
[hey.minyeong@gmail.com](mailto:hey.minyeong@gmail.com). Min will delete the
analytics data associated with that ID unless it must be retained for legal
reasons.

You can manage location access in Chrome and use weather by searching for a
city instead. You can remove locally stored settings and weather data by
clearing Min's data or uninstalling the extension.

## How Min shares information

Min only shares information with Open-Meteo, BigDataCloud, and PostHog for the
features described in this policy. Min does not sell your personal data or use
it for advertising.

## Chrome Web Store requirements

Min uses information from Chrome extension APIs only to provide the features
described in this policy. This use complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data
Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
